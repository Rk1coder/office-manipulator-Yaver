# Visual review checklist (go through every slide, in this order)

1. **Legibility:** any text cut off, overflowing its card/box, running off the slide, or overlapping
   another element? Any garbled or missing characters (Turkish ç ğ ı İ ö ş ü, "?" or boxes)?
2. **Contrast:** any text hard to read against its background (gray on color, light on light, text over a
   busy image without an overlay)?
3. **Geometry:** elements touching the slide edge, misaligned left edges, cards in a row with different
   heights or tops, uneven gutters, connectors crossing text?
4. **Balance:** large empty regions on content slides, or everything crammed into one corner? Is the most
   important element (title claim, key number, highlighted bar) the most visually prominent?
5. **Type:** body text smaller than ~14 pt (or tiny text floating in a large empty box), walls of text
   (> ~60 words)? Does the deck have a typographic voice — the pairing's heading face on titles,
   eyebrow labels / display numbers / lead sentences used consistently — or does it look like default
   Calibri/Cambria? Fonts outside the pairing?
6. **Images and charts:** distorted, pixelated, tiny or missing images; gray placeholders; charts with
   unreadable labels, missing units, or a highlight that doesn't match the title?
7. **Deck consistency:** title position, fonts, palette and margins identical across content slides; dark
   treatment only for cover/dividers/statements/closing; no layout repeated 3+ times in a row.
8. **Story:** do the titles alone tell the story of the plan, in the planned order, with no slide missing?

Report each defect once, on the slide where it occurs, with a concrete fix in inches/points or a clear
instruction. Approve only when a demanding client would present the deck as is.
